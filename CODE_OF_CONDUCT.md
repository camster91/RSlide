# Code of Conduct

## Our promise

We want RSlide to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their background, identity, experience level or role. We promise to make taking part a harassment-free experience for all.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Respect different opinions and experiences.
- Give and take feedback gracefully. Critique ideas, not people.
- Own your mistakes and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the people who use RSlide.

## What is not okay

- Insults, put-downs or personal attacks.
- Harassment of any kind, public or private.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Sharing someone's private information without permission.
- Any behavior that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions and chat — and when you represent the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If you see or experience a problem, contact the maintainer privately through the email listed on [@camster91's GitHub profile](https://github.com/camster91). Reports are kept confidential.

## What happens next

Maintainers will review each report and may respond with a warning, a temporary ban or a permanent ban, depending on how serious it is.
