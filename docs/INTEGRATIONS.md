# Slides, Teams, Zoom and streams

Everything here is in your event's **Settings → Slides and apps** on the host page.

## Any deck: the join slide

**Download PNG** makes a 1920×1080 picture with your join address, code and QR code, in your event's colours. Put it at the start of your deck in PowerPoint, Google Slides, Keynote or Canva.

Then run polls from your phone with the **Remote** (host page → Remote → scan the QR code).

## Google Slides, PowerPoint for the web, Canva: your slides with live polls on top

1. Share your deck so that **anyone with the link can view** it.
   - Google Slides: Share → General access → Anyone with the link → Viewer. Copy the link.
   - PowerPoint for the web: File → Share → Embed → copy the embed code (the whole `<iframe…>` is fine).
   - Canva: Share → More → Embed → copy the link.
2. Paste it in **Present your slides with live polls on top** and press Save.
3. Open the big screen (**Open big screen with slides**). Click the slides once, then use the arrow keys to move through them.

When you launch a poll (from the host page or the remote), its results appear over your slides. Highlighted questions and the leaderboard show up the same way. The join code stays in the corner.

## PowerPoint add-in (Windows, Mac and the web)

The add-in puts a live box on a slide: a poll's results, your Q&A, the leaderboard, or the join code. It can also **start its poll when the slide comes up**, so you never leave PowerPoint.

**Install it once:**

- Just you (PowerPoint on the web or Windows): Home → Add-ins → More add-ins → My add-ins → **Upload My Add-in**, and pick the file from **Get add-in**.
- PowerPoint for Mac: put the file in `~/Library/Containers/com.microsoft.Powerpoint/Data/Documents/wef` (make the folder if it's missing), then restart PowerPoint.
- Your whole organisation: a Microsoft 365 admin uploads the file in the admin center → Settings → Integrated apps → **Upload custom apps**.

**Use it:** Insert → My add-ins → pick it. Paste your **host link**, choose what the slide shows, and press **Show on slide**. During the slide show it updates live.

> Anyone with the presentation file can control your event (the host link is saved in it). Before sharing the file, remove those slides, or make a new host link from the event's settings.

## Microsoft Teams

**Get app** downloads a Teams app package made for your site.

- Upload it: Teams → Apps → Manage your apps → **Upload an app** → Upload a custom app. (Your admin may need to allow custom apps, or upload it for everyone in the Teams admin center.)
- In a meeting: **+ Apps** → pick it → type your event code. Everyone gets a side panel where they ask, vote and answer polls.
- Presenters can press **Show results to everyone** to put the big-screen view on the meeting stage.
- In a channel or chat: **+** (add a tab) → pick it → type your event code.

## Zoom

There's no Zoom app yet (Zoom apps need Zoom Marketplace approval). What works well today:

- Share your screen with the **big screen** page (or your slides with live polls on top).
- Paste the join link in the Zoom chat. People answer on their phone or in a new browser tab.
- Zoom Webinars and live streams: add the **stream overlay** (Settings → Stream and embed) as a browser source in OBS, or use Zoom's "custom live streaming".

## Keynote

Keynote can't show live web content. Use the join slide, run polls with the remote, and switch to the big screen (or mirror it) when you want to show results. Each poll's **More → Copy big-screen link** opens a screen that only ever shows that poll.

## Live streams (OBS, vMix, StreamYard)

Settings → Stream and embed → copy the **stream overlay** link and add it as a browser source (1920×1080). It's see-through, and shows the live poll, highlighted question or leaderboard as a lower third.
